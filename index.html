The Method

Every pick on every board runs through the same process. It's analysis, not action — the number comes first, the market comes second.

Step 1

Model first

A number is built for every game from published models and ratings — efficiency, pace, injuries, rest and matchup — before anyone looks at the betting line. No number, no pick.

Step 2

Shop the price

The model number is compared against the opener and the current line at three books. A pick needs a real edge — 3 points for straight bets, 5 for props — and the best verified price is the one shown.

Step 3

Ranked, not padded

Picks are graded Strong, Lean or No Bet, and only Strong and Lean make the boards. After the close, every pick is graded against the closing line — wins and losses both stay on the record.
