The standard, in one breath: sub-sections always Teams → Players → Touchdown Scorers (football only), same labels on every board. Strong rows get the four-box strip — My Number vs the best book, the edge, and the EV, in one EV style ($ per $1) everywhere. Lean rows are two clean lines: the pick, then the game and time. No book tables, no paragraphs, no units, and no No Bet rows inside Top Picks — the full three-book comparison lives on the game card below, where it already is.
